Section I. No-Consent Mode (Default / No-Tracking)

If you have not clicked the “Submit to Hypnotoad!” button—you are completely invisible in the astral realm. This means:

  1. Hypn0 (HypnoSVG) does not deploy any client identifiers. Neither cookies nor browser localStorage records are created.
  2. External analytics systems are disabled: tracking beacons and metric counters (Google Tag Manager, analytics scripts, etc.) are blocked at the template level and physically never loaded onto the client device.
  3. Server logs—the only baseline telemetry captured by infrast­ructure (IP address, User-Agent, requested URL, and timestamp) — are utilized strictly for DDoS defense and uptime monitoring, retained for a minimal duration with regular rotation faster than memories fade after a hypnosis session.

Section II. Restrictions for Free Humanoids Without a Brain Slug

Humanoids who decline tracking or withhold explicit consent can freely use the local vector generator and browse the gallery. However, they cannot access pseudo-social platform features:

  • Publishing original works to the public “Trance Gallery”;
  • Voting for artworks (transmitting psychic resonance / likes);
  • Submitting complaints or flagging spam and non-transcendent content.

Reason for Restrictions: Anti-Abuse & Bot Defense

To operate the gravit­ational Brain Slug sorting algorithm (curated ranking) and fairly distribute artworks across tiers (“Incubator”, “Approved by Brain Slug”, “Deep Trance Hall of Fame”), the platform requires a unique anonymous client anchor.

Because a humanoid who has not “surrendered to the Hypnotoad” carries no device anchor, the Lord of Mental Intros­pections (the server) cannot recognize duplicate votes. This would lead to instant scripted vote tampering, spam surges, and the destabi­lization of the entire gallery ecosystem.

  1. The browser generates a cryptog­raphic pseudo-random identifier via crypto.randomUUID() (UUID v4).

  3. The lifespan is configured for two Earth years, with transmission flags set to safe mode (path=/; SameSite=Lax).
  4. The hypn0_vid token is a purely random string and cannot be linked to your real identity, physical vessel, or cerebral Theta/Alpha brainwaves.
  5. The identifier is used by the backend exclusively for rate limiting and enforcing the “one vote per artwork per browser” rule.

Section IV. File Processing Security during SVG Generation

  • Input Data Sanitization (Source Images): All inbound images pass through the Pillow mental shredder, decoded exclusively in RAM, and immediately cleansed of mundane metadata. No EXIF tags, geolocation, camera models, or temporary disk artifacts remain on server storage.
  • Output Vector Security: The server stores only vector artworks synthesized by the native Hypn0 (HypnoSVG) core. Output SVGs are generated strictly program­matically and contain no <script> tags, external xlink:href links, or event handlers like onload or onclick. This eliminates Stored XSS risks when viewing other humanoids’ works: foreign halftones are completely harmless to your optic nerves (aside from the risk of spontaneous hypnotic trance).
